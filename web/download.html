Print from your phone to any printer

No more emailing files to your computer. Tap Share → PrintBridge in WhatsApp or Gallery and the document prints on the printer connected to your Windows computer — from home, the office or anywhere else.

Download for Windows (Free)
Windows 10 / 11  •  No additional software required
1

Install on your computer

On the computer connected to your printer, download PrintBridge.exe and run it. The window shows a QR code, a Computer ID and a PIN.

⚠️ If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This is normal for newly published apps.
2

Connect your phone

Open the phone app, tap 📷 Scan QR code and scan the code on your computer, or enter the Computer ID and PIN. Click Allow on the computer and you're connected.

Scan with your phone camera
or open on your phone:
printbridge.onrender.com

3

Share and print

Install the phone app using the "Install" button in the app. Then:

  • In WhatsApp or Gallery, tap Share → PrintBridge
  • Tap Print — done ✅

🤖 Android: print from any app

Install PrintBridge Printer to print bank statements, bills, tickets and web pages straight from the Print option in any app. Your computer's printer appears in Android's printer list.

Download for Android (APK)

When installing, Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser. After installing, open the app and follow the three steps.

🌍

Wi-Fi or mobile data — your document always reaches your printer.

📄

All common formats

PDF, photos, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Multiple files and copies at once.

ID cards & passport photos

Print shop mode

Customers order by scanning a QR code. Manage the queue, pricing and reports from one dashboard.

💻

Computer to computer

Print from one computer to a printer connected to another, over the internet.

🔒

Secure

New devices connect only with your approval. Files are deleted from the server as soon as they print.

🆓

Free

No account and no sign-up. Download and start printing.

Requirements

  • The printer must be connected to the computer and installed in Windows.
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint files require Microsoft Office or LibreOffice on the computer. PDFs and photos print without any extra software.
  • The computer must be switched on with PrintBridge running (it starts automatically with Windows).
  • On iPhone, the Share menu shortcut is not available. Open the app and tap "Choose files" instead.
